# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Parents and families in Argentina evaluating a residential English and adventure camp for adolescents aged 13–17. They need to understand the experience, daily rhythm, safety coordination, destination, dates, and practical next step before requesting availability.

## Product Purpose

Patagonia Summer Camp 2027 is a residential English-immersion and adventure camp in Villa La Angostura, Patagonia, Argentina, running from 4–12 December 2027. The site exists to help families understand and trust the experience, then request availability or further information.

Success means a parent understands what the camp is, who it is for, when and where it happens, and how the program combines language immersion with outdoor experience, and can take the next step of requesting information.

## Positioning

The distinctive mechanism is English learned through the lived routines of a residential outdoor camp rather than through classroom instruction alone. The product joins language immersion, adventure, supervised group life, and the specific setting of Patagonia for adolescents aged 13–17.

## Operating Context

Families evaluate a multi-day residential experience and need practical information about the daily routine, activities, safety and health coordination, destination, age range, dates, and what happens next. The primary language is Spanish for Argentina. The core decision is made by adults on behalf of adolescent participants.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Public informational site built with Next.js App Router, React, Tailwind CSS, Motion for React, and Lenis for desktop smooth-wheel scrolling.
- Public routes cover the home, English program, adolescent program, itinerary and activities, destination, safety and coordination, family guide, FAQ, about, and contact.
- The contact form provides the primary conversion path for availability and family inquiries.
- The camp serves adolescents aged 13–17 and is scheduled for 4–12 December 2027 in Villa La Angostura.
- Prices, availability details, and some operational facts are not yet confirmed and must not be invented.
- Mobile Safari behavior and safe-area correctness are explicit product constraints.
- Product copy is Spanish (Argentina); the site is for web and mobile browsers.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the name **Patagonia Summer Camp 2027** and the existing logo.
- The previous visual direction, palette, typography, and layout may be replaced by a new home-page direction.
- The visual work must make the camp feel specific to Patagonia and to the experience of teenagers living an outdoor English immersion, not like a generic language school or generic adventure camp.
- Prefer the familiar category-standard campaign structure executed with exceptional craft rather than an unconventional art direction; use Outward Bound as the quality benchmark for clarity, confidence, and family trust.
- Do not fabricate testimonials, customer counts, safety certifications, prices, guarantees, press coverage, or institutional proof.

## Product Principles

1. **Earn parental trust with specifics.** Show the real day, environment, responsibilities, coordination, and next step instead of relying on generic adventure claims.
2. **Show English through experience.** Every major program benefit should connect to an activity, routine, or place in which adolescents actually use the language.
3. **Make Patagonia part of the proposition.** The destination should shape the experience and composition, not function only as a photographic background.
4. **Differentiate without exaggeration.** Build a memorable visual and verbal identity from the camp’s real mechanism, without inventing proof or manufacturing urgency.
5. **Design the parent’s decision path, not just the campaign surface.** A striking first viewport must lead into clarity about fit, safety, logistics, and inquiry.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site must remain usable on mobile Safari, with readable type, visible focus, keyboard-operable controls, meaningful alternative text, and content that does not depend on animation to be understood.
