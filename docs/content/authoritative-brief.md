## Core proposition

Patagonia Summer Camp combines nature, local knowledge, outdoor activities, English immersion, and teamwork in Villa La Angostura, Patagonia, Argentina. Each experience is an opportunity to learn by doing, communicate, collaborate, explore, express oneself, overcome challenges, and connect with others.

## Program and dates

- Audience: adolescents aged 13–17 years.
- Format: residential summer camp.
- Planned dates: 4–12 December 2027.
- Location: Villa La Angostura, Neuquén, Patagonia Argentina.
- English is integrated into daily life, activities, conversations, and short participatory English sessions each morning.
- The goal is to build autonomy, confidence, teamwork, and willingness to communicate.

## Philosophy

Growing means exploring, expressing oneself, overcoming challenges, and connecting with others. The program offers a space to discover new possibilities through:

- Adventure and confidence: explore and overcome challenges to gain confidence in oneself and others.
- Learning by doing: live English in real situations, communicate ideas, and discover new perspectives.
- Freedom of expression: be oneself, share ideas, and find different ways to participate.
- Community and respect: create friendships, value differences, and learn to care for one another.
- Body and mind in balance: move the body, care for the mind, and enjoy the outdoors; shared adventure and challenges develop confidence, curiosity, and resilience.

## Ecosystems and communities

Knowing a place changes the way people care for it. Patagonia is nature, communities, stories, and knowledge that deserve to be known and valued. Participants explore the territory with curiosity and respect, meet the people who give it life, and discover why the places they feel close to are worth caring for.

## Residence

- The residence is in Villa La Angostura, 2 km from the town center.
- The property has 26 hectares of park and native forest.
- It has a shoreline on Lake Nahuel Huapi and a sports area.
- Its open spaces combine outdoor activities, rest, and shared experiences in contact with nature.
- It has a large dining room used for meals and group gatherings.
- Final room distribution, included services, and other operational details will be communicated with the definitive program.

- Each activity considers weather conditions and safety requirements.
- Prior outdoor experience is not required in general, but each activity may have specific requirements.
- Final equipment and any activity-specific experience requirements will be communicated before the camp.

## Founder story

Hernán Paulitti is the founder of Patagonia Summer Camp.

- In 2011 he studied English at Instituto de Formación Docente y Técnica N° 55 in Escobar, Buenos Aires.
- He later taught English in Argentina for many years.
- He moved to Bologna, Italy, studied Italian at Academia Lingue, and worked with Italian agritech, design, and technology companies teaching English to architects, designers, operators, and other professionals.
- He organized English-learning trips to Ireland and England for adults and adolescents.
- He worked in northern Italian summer camps as a teacher and activity leader.
- He later worked in Laponia, Finland, collaborating with Finnish tourism companies, guiding visitors under the northern lights and taking huskies into snowy forests at -35°C.
- After spending a week camping in El Chaltén, he promised himself to return to Patagonia and share an experience that helps others venture into other worlds through language.
- Patagonia Summer Camp fulfills that promise by valuing the region through friendship, language, respect for local culture, and curiosity about nature.

## Hobbies and leadership

Hernán enjoys CrossFit, yoga, cycling, music production, the mountains, and outdoor activities. He wants to share that sense of clarity, energy, and authenticity with participating adolescents.

## Family logistics

- Each family arranges and pays for transportation to Villa La Angostura.
- Transportation from other provinces is not included in the program price.
- If a family contracts group transportation from Bariloche airport, it must provide the required travel information.
- The coordination team receives participants at a previously communicated place and time, organizes luggage, presents the coordinators and facilities, and explains coexistence rules.
- Before arrival, families provide guardian contacts, travel information and estimated arrival time, participation and transfer authorizations, and medical, dietary, and emergency contact information.
- Final pickup and return arrangements will be communicated before the camp.

## Health, medication, and safety

- Families must disclose allergies, medical conditions, medication and instructions, dietary restrictions, activity restrictions, medical coverage, and emergency contacts.
- Medication must arrive identified and accompanied by clear instructions.
- The storage, administration, and supervision procedure will be communicated before the camp.
- Activities will use appropriate providers and professionals whose qualifications, insurance, protocols, and operating conditions are verified.
- An emergency procedure covering family communication, access to medical assistance, and the responsibilities of coordinators and providers will be defined before the camp.
- Families will be contacted as soon as possible when a medical or safety situation requires it.
- Required medical coverage, insurance scope, and the procedure for medical consultations, accidents, and emergencies will be communicated before travel.

## Devices, food, and coexistence

- Device-use rules will favor participation, rest, and face-to-face interaction while preserving family communication; final hours and custody rules will be communicated before the camp.
- Meals will be appropriate for adolescents and organized around physical activity.
- Dietary and nutritional needs will be handled with care and confidentiality after advance disclosure and confirmation of what can be accommodated.
- Participants are expected to respect peers, staff, and providers; follow safety instructions; care for facilities, equipment, and nature; respect schedules and coexistence rules; avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or violence; and not consume alcohol, illegal drugs, tobacco, or other conduct that compromises safety or wellbeing.
- Incidents are handled prioritizing everyone's safety, with family involvement when required by the participation conditions communicated beforehand.
- Participants should bring only a moderate amount of money for small personal expenses if needed. Valuables, expensive devices, jewelry, and unnecessary objects should not be brought. Procedures for money, valuables, and lost property will be communicated before the camp.
- Emotional discomfort or difficulty adapting to residential life will be handled with close support, respecting each participant's pace and involving the family when needed.
