# Home — Surface Brief

## Scope and visitor mode

- Target: `app/(site)/page.tsx` and only the shared components required by the Home.
- Mode: Persuade.
- Scope: complete Home redesign; inner routes remain unchanged.
- Build path: code-led; no image-generation tool is available.
- Audit reference: Outward Bound for clarity, confidence, and family trust.
- Taste Skill direction sheet was dealt, but the user explicitly chose the canonical category execution. Taste Skill remains the house-style and visual-inspection gate.

## Audience, job, action, proof and constraints

- Primary audience: parents and families evaluating a residential camp for adolescents aged 13–17.
- Job: understand the experience, trust the coordination and daily life, and request availability.
- Primary action: “Consultar disponibilidad”.
- Secondary action: “Conocer el programa”.
- Proof available: real dates, age range, destination, program detail, founder history, 26-hectare reference environment, and a mixed photographic library.
- Proof gaps: no confirmed prices, availability inventory, testimonials, customer metrics, press, or certifications.
- Constraints: preserve name and logo, existing claims, routes, CTAs, JSON-LD, accessibility, iPhone Safari geometry, and honest labeling of provisional imagery.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

- Direction: the category-standard family camp campaign executed at Outward Bound’s level of clarity and confidence.
- Memorable moment: a concise “one week as a route” strip in the English-immersion section, showing the real progression from activity to language use without turning the page into an interactive map.
- Differentiator: specificity and photographic honesty rather than an unusual art world.

## Direction contract

### THESIS

A precise, warm and confident family-camp campaign that makes the mechanism—English learned through real outdoor routines in Patagonia—immediately intelligible without novelty theatre.

### OWN-WORLD

Deep forest and glacial-lake colors, warm natural paper, real photography as evidence, bold practical typography, generous editorial spacing, and clearly bounded information fields. The interface is structured like a well-designed camp prospectus: decisive hierarchy, concrete facts, reassuring confidence and no decorative clutter.

### STORY

The parent first sees what the camp is, who it is for and when it happens; then follows how a real day turns activity, language and shared responsibility into confidence; finally sees the residence, program, safety coordination and a direct availability action.

### FIRST VIEWPORT

A photographic Hero occupies roughly half the viewport. The H1 states the mechanism in no more than two lines, with a supporting sentence and one primary action. A compact fact strip makes age, dates, residential format and location legible. The navbar and content are fully visible on mobile Safari.

### FORM

Canonical family summer-camp marketing page: photographic hero, essential facts, benefit narrative, program mechanism, founder story, environment gallery, adolescent-program module, safety reassurance and closing inquiry path.
